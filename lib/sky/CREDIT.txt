stars.bin: the Yale Bright Star Catalogue, 5th revised edition (Hoffleit and Warren, 1991). Public domain.
http://tdc-www.harvard.edu/catalogs/bsc5.html
9,096 stars, brightest first. Eight bytes each, little-endian: right ascension (0..65535 for 0..360 degrees),
declination (-32767..32767 for -90..90 degrees), V magnitude x 1000, B-V colour x 1000 (0.6 where the catalogue gives none).
